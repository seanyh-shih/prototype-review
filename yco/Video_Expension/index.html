What Is Video Expansion?

AI Video Expansion generates new visual content around your original footage, helping you change aspect ratios without cropping your shot.

Why Use Video Expansion

One Video, More Places to Post: Reuse footage you already have on TikTok, YouTube, your website or your product listings instead of reshooting it.

  • Frame It Your Way: Drag your footage to where you want it in the new frame, or, when it already has the ratio you pick, scale it down to leave room around it.
  • Expand the Part You Need: Upload your video, then select a 1–30-second segment to expand.
  • Keep Creating While It Renders: Keep editing or start another video while one generates. Every finished video is saved to History and My Gallery.

How to Expand a Video Online

Upload, set the new frame, then generate and download.

STEP 1

Upload Your Video

Upload an MP4, MOV or M4V file, or choose one from My Gallery. Then select a 1–30-second segment to expand.

STEP 2

Choose an Aspect Ratio and Reframe

Pick the ratio your platform needs, then drag your video to place it in the new frame.

STEP 3

Generate & Download

Select Generate. The finished video appears in History, where you can preview it and download it.
